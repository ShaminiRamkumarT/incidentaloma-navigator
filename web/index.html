IncidentaLens

Incidentaloma follow-up checker for kidney and adrenal lesions on CT

Paste a CT report. The app finds incidental kidney and adrenal lesions, applies Bosniak v2019, ACR 2018 and ESE/ENSAT 2023, and flags follow-up advice that is too weak or more than needed.

Prototype · fictional data · not for clinical use

Results update as you type. Try editing a number: in the first sample, change 7 mm mural nodule to thin wall, or change a recommendation to Urology referral.

Every sample report run through the checker, sorted so the most serious gaps come first. Select a row to open that report.

Flags. Under-call: the guideline asks for something the report leaves out (high severity when a specialist referral is missing). Over-call: the report asks for follow-up the guideline does not. Any imaging meets an imaging requirement, and a referral covers one.

Sources: Silverman SG et al., Radiology 2019;292:475–488 · Herts BR et al., J Am Coll Radiol 2018;15:264–273 · Fassnacht M et al., Eur J Endocrinol 2023;189:G1–G42. Simplified: CT only, no patient history, no adrenal washout.

The reader is keyword-based and works best on reports written like the samples: sizes in cm and attenuation written as "HU". It runs entirely in your browser, and nothing you paste is sent anywhere.
